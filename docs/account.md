# Account

## Create an account

You can create an account with an email address and a password, or sign in with Google. Business accounts need a company registration number.

## Reset your password

Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email address. You will get a reset link that is valid for 1 hour.

## Change delivery address

You can change the delivery address of a parcel until it is handed to the courier. Open the parcel in your account and choose "Change address". After the parcel is with the courier, the address can no longer be changed.

## Delete your account

You can delete your account from the Privacy section of your settings. Deletion is permanent and takes effect within 30 days. Open orders must be completed or cancelled first.

## Support hours

Human support agents are available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 local time. This bot answers questions at any time.
