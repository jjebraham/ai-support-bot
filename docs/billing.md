# Billing and payments

## Payment methods

Parcelo accepts Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and bank transfer. Business accounts can also pay by monthly invoice.

## Invoices

Invoices are available as PDF on the Billing page of your account. A new invoice is created on the first day of each month for business accounts.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, the order stays on hold for 24 hours. Update your card on the Billing page and the payment is retried automatically. After 24 hours the order is cancelled.

## Currency

All prices are shown and charged in EUR. Your bank may add its own fee for currency conversion.
