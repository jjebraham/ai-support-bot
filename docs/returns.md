# Returns and refunds

## Return window

You can return a parcel within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## How to return

Create a return label from the Returns page in your account, print it and drop the parcel at any Parcelo pickup point. Return labels are free for orders over 50 EUR, otherwise a return costs 3.90 EUR.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the return is scanned at our warehouse. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged.

## Damaged items

If an item arrives damaged, take photos of the parcel and the item and open a damage claim within 7 days of delivery. Approved damage claims get a full refund including shipping fees.
