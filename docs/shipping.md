# Shipping

Parcelo is a fictional parcel delivery service used as sample data for this demo.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the country. Express delivery takes 1 business day if the order is placed before 14:00 local time. Orders placed after 14:00 ship the next business day.

## Shipping prices

Standard delivery costs 4.90 EUR per parcel up to 5 kg. Express delivery costs 12.90 EUR per parcel up to 5 kg. Parcels between 5 kg and 20 kg add 1.50 EUR per extra kilogram.

## International shipping

Parcelo ships to all EU countries. International delivery takes 5 to 9 business days. Customs duties for non-EU destinations are paid by the recipient.

## Restricted items

Parcelo does not ship lithium batteries sold on their own, aerosols, flammable liquids or live animals. Batteries installed inside a device are allowed.
