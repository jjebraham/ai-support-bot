# Tracking

## Tracking number

Every parcel gets a tracking number that starts with PCL followed by 10 digits. The tracking number is sent by email as soon as the label is printed.

## Tracking status

You can track a parcel on the Parcelo website or by sending the tracking number to this bot. Tracking updates appear within 2 hours of each scan.

## Missing parcel

If tracking shows "delivered" but you did not receive the parcel, check with neighbours and the building reception first. If it is still missing after 48 hours, open a missing parcel claim from your account page. Claims are reviewed within 5 business days.
